Replay: the learned sequence, played fast during each ripple

    
CA3 stores a sequence of 10 groups of cells: group 1 → 2 → … → 10.
      In each chart, rows are the 10 groups (group 1 at the bottom) and time runs left to right; bright = firing.
      The dots mark when each group fired on average, inside the ripple (dashed lines).
      Forward replay: the dots climb (1 first, 10 last), ρ near +1.
      Reverse replay: the dots fall (10 first, 1 last), ρ near −1.
      In this model all groups fire in one burst, so the order is squeezed into a few milliseconds:
      the lower chart zooms in on it. Click a card to jump there in the spike view.
